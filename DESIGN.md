# Design System: Budget Tracker

## Overview

**Creative North Star: "The Pocket Bank"**

This is a consumer banking app, not a document. It behaves like the thing people already have in their pocket: a balance that leads at display scale, a stack of tappable rows underneath it, circular category avatars doing the recognition work, and every surface a flat rounded card floating on a near-black or pure-white ground. Nothing is ruled, framed, or tabulated. There is one vivid blue that carries every action, and colour is never decorative.

The density is generous and thumb-first. Cards are 20px-rounded and separated by air, not by lines. Rows are 44px-plus and hoverable. Controls are pills. Entry arrives from the bottom edge on a phone and from the centre on a desktop. Weight, not case or rule, makes hierarchy: Manrope runs 400 through 800 and the heavy end is reserved for money.

The world is pinned to Revolut and played straight, with one deliberate departure: **no gradients anywhere**, including where the reference uses them. Depth is done entirely with surface tone and radius. Confirmed visual rejections, carried from the build it replaced: hairline rules as the structural device, ruled column tables, uppercase letter-spaced micro-labels, monospace "stamp" text, square corners, and paper tints.

**Key Characteristics:**
- Flat rounded cards as the only container; zero gradients, verified live
- A display-scale balance in Manrope 800 with tight negative tracking
- Circular category avatars in flat, full-strength category colour
- Pill controls; the selected segment fills with the foreground ink
- Colour means exactly three things: identity, money in, trouble
- One navigation at every width — sidebar from `lg`, tab bar below it
- One authored motion moment: a new row dropping onto the stack

## Colors

Two full themes — a pure-white day and a near-black night — sharing one semantic vocabulary, where every non-neutral hue is doing a job rather than decorating one.

### Primary
- **Vivid Blue** (`#1f5eff` light / `#5b8bff` dark): the single accent. It carries the primary button, the centre quick action, "see all" links, the active tab-bar item, complete bars in the six-month trend, the focus ring, the caret and the selection highlight. Nothing else earns it. Measured contrast: 5.12:1 on white, 4.69:1 on `surface`, 6.13:1 in dark.
- **Accent Soft** (`#e6edff` / `#1a2440`): the accent at wash strength, used only to mark a drop target while reordering categories.

### Secondary
- **Arrival Green** (`#06753c` / `#2fd07a`): money that came in — income figures and the "in" line under the balance. It never marks health, progress, or success in general. Measured 5.81:1 light, 8.82:1 dark.
- **Trouble Red** (`#d4342f` / `#ff6b66`): something is wrong — a field error, a destructive action, an over-budget bar and its verdict. Ordinary spending is never red. Measured 4.85:1 light, 6.38:1 dark.

### Tertiary
- **Warning Amber** (`#a75c00` / `#f0a92e`): the one middle state — a budget running ahead of the month's pace, not yet over. Its `soft` companion exists but is currently unused in the interface.

### Neutral
- **Ground** (`#ffffff` / `#0b0c0f`): the page. Pure white by day, near-black by night; the theme flips the whole ramp rather than tinting it.
- **Raised** (`#ffffff` / `#16181d`): the card fill. In light it equals the ground, which is exactly why light-mode cards need their hairline; in dark it lifts one step off the ground and needs nothing.
- **Surface / Surface-2 / Surface-3** (`#f4f5f7`, `#eaecf0`, `#dfe2e8` / `#16181d`, `#202329`, `#2b2f37`): the three tonal steps that do all the work borders and shadows would otherwise do. Surface is the row hover; Surface-2 fills inputs, secondary buttons, segment tracks and avatar wells; Surface-3 is the hover above that, the progress track, the grab handle and the scrollbar thumb.
- **Ink** (`#0b0c0f` / `#ffffff`): primary text, and the fill of a selected segment or a toast — the one inversion in the system.
- **Muted Ink** (`#61646e` / `#9ba0ac`): secondary lines — row sublabels, card meta, field labels, unselected controls. Measured 5.91:1 light, 6.78:1 dark.
- **Faint Ink** (`#6e727d` / `#8f95a1`): the quietest tier — hints, placeholders, percentages, inactive tab items, icon-only affordances at rest. Measured 4.81:1 light, 5.90:1 dark.

### Category Palette
Fourteen flat, saturated hues offered in the category picker (values in the sidecar). They are user data, not brand: they appear at full strength in avatars, ring slices, legend dots and budget bar fills, and they are never tinted, shaded, or reused as UI colour. An uncategorised or collapsed "Other" entry falls back to a neutral grey (`#8b8f9a`).

### Named Rules
**The Three Meanings Rule.** Colour says exactly three things in this app and no more: a category colour is identity, green means money arrived, red means trouble. Any fourth meaning is a bug. Audit test: if a swatch on screen can't be assigned to one of those three, remove it.

**The Sign Carries Direction Rule.** An expense is written `−€12.40` in default ink, not in red. The minus sign carries the direction so red stays free to mean trouble. Only a positive amount takes a colour, and only green.

**The Measured Floor Rule.** Every text token is measured against its own ground and clears 4.5:1 in both themes. No text colour ships on an estimate, and no meaning is carried by colour alone — status is always also written in words.

## Typography

**Display Font:** Manrope (with `ui-sans-serif`, `system-ui`, `-apple-system`, `Segoe UI`, `sans-serif`)
**Body Font:** Manrope — the same family
**Label/Mono Font:** none; there is no monospace anywhere in this system

**Character:** One geometric sans doing everything, loaded at 400–800 with the `ss01` stylistic set on. Its heavy end is unusually solid for a grotesque, which is why the money is set at 800: figures read as objects, not as text. Tight negative tracking at the large sizes keeps a long balance compact.

### Hierarchy
- **Display** (800, 40px → 52px from `sm`, line-height 1, tracking −0.035em): the account balance, and nothing else. One per screen at most.
- **Headline** (800, 24px, tracking −0.03em): the page title in the sticky header. The modal title (19px) and the sidebar wordmark (19px) sit one step down on the same setting.
- **Title** (800, 17px, tracking −0.02em): card headings and empty-state headings.
- **Body** (700, 15px): the primary line of a row — a transaction's label, a category's name, a budget's name. Amounts on the same line go to 800.
- **Meta** (400–600, 13px, muted ink): the second line of a row, card meta, verdicts.
- **Label** (700, 13px, muted ink, sentence case): form field labels. **Micro** (600, 11–12px) is the floor: tab-bar labels, percentages, hints.

### Named Rules
**The One Family Rule.** Manrope is the only typeface in the system. There is no serif, no monospace, no second sans. Weight (400/500/600/700/800) and size carry every distinction that a family change would otherwise carry.

**The Tabular Money Rule.** Every money value goes through the `.figure` treatment — tabular lining numerals and −0.02em tracking — so a balance never reflows as its digits change. If a number is currency, it is tabular; if it is not currency, it is not.

**The Weight-Not-Case Rule.** Hierarchy is made with weight and size only. No uppercase letter-spaced micro-labels, no small caps, no letter-spaced eyebrows. Sentence case everywhere, including buttons and field labels.

### The category palette

Colour is identity in this system, so the fourteen category colours are part of it, not decoration chosen per-category by a user. They live in `CATEGORY_COLORS` (`src/lib/constants.ts`) and are used at **full strength, flat**, in avatars, ring slices and budget-bar fills — never tinted, never as a background wash. `category-none` is the role for a row whose category was deleted and for the collapsed "Other" ring slice; it is a named token, not a grey picked by hand.

Category colour never appears in the semantic layer. A budget bar carries its category's colour while on track, and switches to `caution` or `negative` when the verdict changes — the swap is the signal.

## Layout

A two-part shell: a 240px sidebar rail from `lg` up, and everything else in a single column. Content is centred in a `max-width` of 768px, widening to 1024px at `lg`. Page gutters are 16px on a phone and 24px from `sm`; the vertical stack rhythm between cards is a constant 16px, and grids inside a page go two-up at `lg` and never earlier.

The header is sticky, transparent-to-90%-ground with a backdrop blur, and carries the page title plus its controls. On a phone the controls drop to their own row beneath the title rather than squeezing it. The tab bar is sticky at the bottom with a top border in `surface-2` and respects `env(safe-area-inset-bottom)`.

Density inside a card: 16px padding, 20px from `sm`. Rows are 12px-gapped horizontally (avatar → text → amount → menu) and 10px-padded vertically, giving a natural 64px row. Internal groups step 4 / 8 / 12 / 16 / 20 / 24px.

### Named Rules
**The One Navigation Rule.** Exactly one navigation is visible at every width: the sidebar from `lg`, the bottom tab bar below it. There is no band where the app shows both and none where it shows neither.

**The 44px Tap Rule.** Any control a thumb aims at gets a 44px minimum hit area, delivered by an invisible centred pseudo-element rather than by inflating the visual control. A 36px icon button stays 36px and still catches a thumb.

## Elevation & Depth

This system is flat by construction. There is no elevation ramp, no ambient shadow, and — verified live on the running app — **zero elements with any `background-image`**. Depth is done with two materials only: a step in surface tone, and a radius. A card reads as raised in dark because its fill sits one step above the ground; in light it reads as raised because a 6%-alpha hairline separates a white card from a white page. That hairline is the only box-shadow present on a default screen.

Two transient surfaces are the sole exceptions, and both are overlays rather than page furniture.

### Shadow Vocabulary
- **Card hairline, light only** (`box-shadow: 0 0 0 1px rgb(11 12 15 / 0.06)`): the separation white cards need from a white page. It must not be applied in dark mode, where fill alone separates.
- **Overflow menu** (`box-shadow: 0 10px 30px -8px rgb(var(--scrim) / 0.35)`): the row and category action menus, which float free of any card.
- **Toast** (`box-shadow: 0 8px 28px -8px rgb(var(--scrim) / 0.35)`): the same lift, one notch softer, for the transient notice.
- **Scrim** (`rgb(var(--scrim) / 0.55)`): the modal backdrop — pure black in dark, near-black ink in light.

### Named Rules
**The No-Gradient Rule.** No gradient anywhere: not on a background, not on a button, not on a chart fill, not as a fade-out mask. This is an explicit brand constraint that holds even where the reference world uses gradients. Audit test: every element's computed `background-image` is `none`.

**The Flat-Ground Rule.** Depth comes from surface tone and radius. If a surface needs to read as lifted, move it a step along the surface ramp — do not reach for a shadow.

**The Hairline-In-Light-Only Rule.** The card ring exists to solve white-on-white. Dark mode must never inherit it.

## Shapes

Everything is rounded and nothing is square. The scale runs 12px (menu items, legend rows, small inner blocks) → 14px (inputs, selects, sidebar nav items, tooltips) → 16px (list rows, popover menus) → 20px (cards, the one container) → 24px (centred dialog) → 28px (the bottom sheet's top corners) → 999px (pills and circles). Borders are essentially absent: one hairline ring on light-mode cards and one top border on the tab bar. Everything else is separated by fill or by air.

Circles carry a specific job. A full circle means either a category avatar (32 / 44 / 56px, filled flat with the category's own colour, white icon at 2.25 stroke) or a round action — the 56px quick actions under the balance, the 36px icon buttons in a row, the close button on a sheet. Pills carry every horizontal control: buttons, segmented tracks, the period picker, the transfer tag, the progress bar and its fill.

### Named Rules
**The No Square Corner Rule.** Nothing in the interface has a 0px corner. The smallest radius in the system is 12px, and even the 2px-high progress bar is fully rounded at both ends.

**The Circle Means Identity Rule.** A filled circle is either a category's identity mark or a round action target. It is never a decorative dot, a bullet, or a badge — the one exception is the 10px legend swatch, which is the ring's category colour repeated.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Shape:** fully pill (999px), never rounded-rectangle.
- **Sizes:** small 36px / medium 44px / large 52px tall, at 14 / 15 / 16px and weight 700.
- **Primary:** accent fill, accent-foreground text, 20px side padding. Reserved for the one committing action in a view.
- **Secondary (default):** `surface-2` fill, primary ink. **Ghost:** muted ink, no fill until hover. **Danger:** negative fill, white text — for destructive confirmation only.
- **Hover / Active:** filled variants drop to 90% opacity on hover and 80% on press; tonal variants step one surface up. Every button also scales to 0.98 on press, over 150ms.
- **Disabled:** 40% opacity, pointer events off. Loading swaps the leading icon for a spinner and sets `aria-busy`.

### Quick Actions
The signature affordance under the balance: a 56px filled disc with a 2.5-stroke icon and a 12px semibold label beneath. The primary verb takes the accent fill; the others take `surface-2`. The disc scales to 1.05 on hover and 0.95 on press. Three at most, in a row that scrolls horizontally on a phone and right-aligns at `lg`.

### Chips
- **Transfer tag:** a `surface-2` pill at 12px semibold muted ink, sitting inline after a row's label. Its job is to say "this moved no money", so it is never coloured.
- **Segmented pill:** a `surface-2` track at 4px padding holding 36px pills; the selected segment fills with the **foreground ink** and takes the ground colour for text. Radio semantics, so arrow keys move between segments.

### Cards / Containers
- **Corner Style:** 20px, the system's signature radius.
- **Background:** `raised`. **Border:** none in dark; a 6% hairline ring in light only.
- **Shadow Strategy:** see Elevation — none, by rule.
- **Internal Padding:** 16px, 20px from `sm`; a `flush` variant drops padding entirely for cards that host a full-bleed list.
- **Head:** a 17px/800 title with an optional 13px muted meta line beneath and a right-aligned action, usually an accent "See all" link.

### Inputs / Fields
- **Style:** filled, not outlined — `surface-2` at 14px radius, 48px tall, 15px medium text, no border at any state.
- **Hover / Focus:** hover steps to `surface-3`; focus drops the fill to `surface` and takes the global 2px accent focus ring at 2px offset.
- **Error:** the fill turns `negative-soft` and a 12px semibold negative message appears below with `role="alert"`.
- **Labels:** 13px bold muted ink above the control, with a plain lowercase "required" marker rather than an asterisk.
- **Select:** the same control with a faint 16px chevron pinned right.

### Navigation
- **Sidebar (`lg` and up):** a 240px rail, 15px bold items at 14px radius, muted at rest, `surface-2` fill plus primary ink when active. The active item also thickens its icon stroke from 2 to 2.4. A faint 12px footnote sits at the bottom.
- **Tab bar (below `lg`):** five 56px items, icon over an 11px bold label, faint ink at rest and **accent** when active, with the icon stroke going to 2.5. Sticky, blurred, with a `surface-2` top border.
- **Period picker:** a `surface-2` pill holding a chevron, a borderless month select, and a chevron — one shared control appearing in the header of every surface that reads a month.

### Modal / Bottom Sheet
- **Phone:** anchored to the bottom edge, 28px top corners, a 36×4px `surface-3` grab handle, sliding up over 320ms. Max height 92dvh with the body scrolling internally.
- **`sm` and up:** a centred dialog at 24px radius, max-width 384 / 448px, rising 10px into place. The grab handle is hidden.
- Backdrop is the scrim at 55%, fading in over 180ms. Focus is trapped, scroll is locked, Escape closes.

### Transaction Row
The unit the whole app is built from: a 44px category avatar, then a 15px bold label with an optional transfer tag, then a 13px muted "category · date" line, then a right-aligned 15px/800 amount, then a 36px overflow button. Hover fills the row `surface` at 16px radius. A just-added row plays the drop animation.

### Pace Bar
The signature data mark: an 8px fully-rounded track in `surface-3` with a fill in the category's own colour, plus a 2px foreground tick at the position of "how much of the month has gone". The fill switches to caution when spending is ahead of pace and to negative when over the limit — status overrides identity, and only here. The width transition runs 500ms ease-out. The verdict is always also written in words beside it.

### Charts
Two Recharts surfaces, both drawn in system tokens and both with library animation switched off.
- **Spending ring:** a 176px donut at 70% inner radius, 6px corner radius, 3° padding between slices, no stroke, starting at 12 o'clock. Category colour is the only encoding, so the total sits in the middle and a tappable legend sits beneath; hovering a slice dims the others to 25%.
- **Trend bars:** six months of spending, fully-rounded bars capped at 30px, accent-filled — except the running month, which is drawn in `surface-3` so a partial bar can never be mistaken for a complete one. Axis ticks are faint 12px semibold; the tooltip is an inverted ink card at 14px radius.

### Feedback
- **Toast:** an inverted card (ink fill, ground text) at 18px radius, bottom-left on a phone above the tab bar, bottom-right from `sm`. Error variant flips to negative fill with white text. An action button is a translucent white pill; offering one doubles the dwell time.
- **Skeleton:** the shape of the list that's coming — a 44px circle, two bars, a right-aligned bar — pulsing `surface-2` between full and 55% opacity over 1.5s.
- **Empty state:** a centred faint icon in a `surface-2` disc, a 17px/800 title, a 14px muted line at `max-w-xs`, and pill actions.

### Motion
Everything eases out with `cubic-bezier(0.16, 1, 0.3, 1)` and nothing overshoots. `fade-in` 180ms for scrims; `rise` 280ms (10px) for page and menu entrances; `sheet-up` 320ms for the bottom sheet; state transitions 150ms. All of it collapses under `prefers-reduced-motion`.

**The One Motion Moment Rule.** The app has exactly one authored animation: `drop` — 420ms, from −14px and 0.98 scale, played on a newly added transaction row as it lands on the stack. Everything else is either a state transition or an entrance. Do not add a second signature moment; add to this one.

**The Sheet-Then-Dialog Rule.** Entry comes from the bottom edge on a phone and from the centre from `sm` up. Both are the same component; never build a separate mobile form.

### Recorded gaps
Honest state of the build, recorded so a future pass fixes them rather than copying them:
- **The amount field is an ordinary text input.** It is styled up (56px tall, 22px/800, currency symbol as a leading adornment) but the reference world's signature keypad-style entry is not built.
- **Rows have hover but no press state.** The premise of the surface is a tappable row, and `:active` feedback on a row is missing — buttons have it, rows do not.
- **Desktop is largely a widened phone.** Only the balance card has a genuinely desktop composition (a two-column grid with the quick actions pulled right). The rest is the same column at a larger `max-width`.

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do:
- **Do** put every new container in a flat 20px card on the surface ramp, with no border in dark and the 6% hairline in light.
- **Do** run every money value through the tabular `.figure` treatment, and set it at weight 800.
- **Do** let the minus sign carry direction on an expense, and reserve green for money arriving.
- **Do** give a category its own colour at full strength in avatars, ring slices and budget fills — flat, never tinted.
- **Do** give every thumb target a 44px hit area with the invisible tap helper rather than by growing the control.
- **Do** state status in words as well as colour — "€40 over", "38% of the month gone" — never in colour alone.
- **Do** keep exactly one navigation visible at every width.
- **Do** open entry as a bottom sheet below `sm` and a centred dialog above it, from the one Modal component.

### Don't:
- **Don't** use a gradient anywhere — background, button, chart fill, or fade mask. This holds even where the pinned reference uses one.
- **Don't** add a shadow to make something read as raised; step the surface tone instead. The only shadows in the system are the light-mode card hairline, the overflow menu, and the toast.
- **Don't** colour ordinary spending red. Red means trouble: errors, destructive actions, over-budget.
- **Don't** introduce a second typeface, a monospace, or uppercase letter-spaced micro-labels.
- **Don't** ship a square corner; the floor is 12px.
- **Don't** rule the interface — no hairline dividers as structure, no column tables, no bordered inputs.
- **Don't** reveal an action only on hover; a touch device has no hover to reveal it with.
- **Don't** draw a partial month's bar the same as a complete one, or let a clamped percentage stand in for the true figure in assistive text.
- **Don't** add a second signature animation. There is one drop, and it belongs to a new row landing.
